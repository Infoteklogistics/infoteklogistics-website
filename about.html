About Infotek

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn’t.

Infoteklogistics exists to close that gap: putting skilled technicians to work for the companies that need them, and giving newly trained talent the field experience that gets them hired.

Our story

Started in the field.

Infotek began in 2018, putting technicians on IT field jobs for national service providers across New York City.

Over the next several months our technicians completed paid work orders for 16 IT service providers: cabling at bank offices, POS installs in department stores, hardware in medical practices, network gear in restaurants. 21 technicians earned paid field work through us.

We saw the same pattern on every job. Employers needed reliable technicians fast. Good people with fresh training couldn’t get their first field job without experience. Today we’re building Infotek around closing that gap.

See the work

Our logo tells the story.

The I is the technician. The L is the employer. The T is Infotek, spanning the gap and joining them.

Leadership

Bolaji Saibu

Founder, CEO and Manager

Bolaji is a Nigerian-American IT and data center professional based in Brooklyn. He came into technology through a New York City tech training program, earning his CompTIA A+, Network+ and Security+, and went on to build a career in IT infrastructure and technology leadership.

Before tech, he worked as a youth advocate and educator. That mix, hands-on technical skills and a belief that people rise when someone opens the door, is why Infotek exists.

The team

A small, hands-on team that covers every part of a job:

  • Finance and systems: billing, contractor pay and the tools that track every job
  • Operations and talent: recruiting, vetting and onboarding technicians
  • Field operations: dispatch, lead-tech support and close-out on site
How we work

Local. Accountable. Documented.

Local

Technicians who live and work in New York City and New Jersey. Shorter drives, faster arrivals, people who know the buildings.

Accountable

Every job has an owner, from intake to invoice. If something slips, we say so, and we say what happens next.

Documented

Photos, a signed report and the invoice the same day, and per-technician outcomes for our training partners.
