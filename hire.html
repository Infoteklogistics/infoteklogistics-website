Questions

Before you request a tech.

Who does the technician work for?

Infotek. Technicians work under agreement with us, and we pay them. You get one invoice from Infotek.

How fast can you start?

We confirm availability within one business day of your request. Start dates depend on the role and any site onboarding you require.

How are technicians vetted?

We check skills against your role, verify ID and confirm references before anyone is sent. Tell us about any site-specific requirements and we’ll match to them.

What if the technician isn’t a good fit?

Tell us and we’ll send a replacement. Your Infotek contact handles it so you don’t start over.

Is there a minimum?

Tell us what you need and we’ll confirm minimum hours for the role before anything is scheduled.
