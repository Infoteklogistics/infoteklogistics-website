Questions

Before we talk.

Are technicians paid?

Yes. Technicians are paid for every hour of field work.

Who supervises technicians on site?

An experienced Infotek lead tech. Newer techs start paired with a lead before working on their own.

What if a technician isn’t ready for the field?

Every technician goes through a readiness check at onboarding. If someone needs more time, we tell you early so you can support them.

What does a partnership cost?

It depends on cohort size and hours. We’ll walk you through options on a call and put it in writing before you commit.

Where is the work?

Mostly across New York, New Jersey and Connecticut, close to where your technicians live, on sites in IT support, POS, network, data center and low-voltage cabling.
