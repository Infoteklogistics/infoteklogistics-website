Questions

Before you send a ticket.

How fast can you get someone on site?

Standard tickets go out next business day. Scheduled rollouts are booked ahead. For same-day requests, call us; we’ll tell you honestly whether a tech is available.

Is there a minimum?

Yes. A visit is billed at a 4-hour minimum. Scope and price are agreed before we dispatch.

Can you work in our ticketing system?

Yes. We check in and out, upload photos and close out in your portal or platform, the same day.

Do you work after hours?

Yes, nights and weekends for store and office work. The after-hours rate is quoted before we schedule.

What paperwork do you need from us?

A scope of work or ticket, site contact and access details. We’ll send our W-9 and sign your subcontract before the first dispatch.
