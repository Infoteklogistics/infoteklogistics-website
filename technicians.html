Questions

Before you sign up.

Do you charge a fee to join?

No. We never charge technicians a fee, for joining, onboarding or getting matched.

How much does it pay?

Most jobs pay $20 to $25 an hour, depending on the work and your experience. The exact pay is in writing before you accept a job.

How do I get paid, and what about taxes?

You work as an independent contractor (1099) and get paid electronically for every hour worked. We confirm the pay schedule at onboarding. As a contractor you handle your own taxes, and we send you a 1099.

Do I need experience?

Not much. If you have hardware or networking basics or a recent training certificate, we’ll match you to work at your level, with a lead tech beside you.

Do I need my own tools?

Each job lists what you need before you accept. Most jobs need basic hand tools and a smartphone.

I’m in a training program. Can I join?

Yes. And if your program wants to partner with us for a whole cohort, send them to our training partners page.
