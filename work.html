Banking, retail & restaurants · NYC

Five months on a national provider’s busiest NYC program

32 work orders · Aug–Dec 2018

The need

A national IT services provider needed reliable local technicians for a steady stream of tickets across New York City.

How we worked

Each job ran through the provider’s own work-order system: check-in and check-out, their checklist, their sign-off.

The work

  • Low-voltage cabling and network work
  • Hardware installs, configuration and refreshes
  • POS installs
  • Sites included a global bank’s offices, a security services company, department store locations and quick-service restaurants

Why it matters

The provider kept sending work for five straight months. Repeat dispatch is the clearest vote of confidence an MSP can give.

Healthcare · NYC

Hardware installs inside working medical practices

6 work orders · Aug & Nov 2018

The need

National field-service providers needed technicians who could work in clinics without disrupting patients.

How we worked

Jobs came through a national field-service platform and a health-services provider in Brooklyn, each closed out to the provider’s requirements.

The work

  • Workstation and hardware installs at medical practices
  • Work scheduled around patient hours
  • Clean, quiet work in clinical spaces
